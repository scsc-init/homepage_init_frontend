### Chapter 2. Members

**Article 4 (Membership Categories)**  
① Members are classified as follows:

1. Associate members
2. Full members
3. Inactive members
4. OB (alumni members)

**Article 5 (Associate Members)**  
① A person joining without an existing membership qualification becomes an associate member.  
② Associate members have the following rights:

1. To freely access the club room
2. To use club room equipment
3. Other rights specified in the operating policies

③ Associate members have the following obligations:

1. To keep the club room clean
2. To avoid damaging club room equipment
3. To participate faithfully in club activities
4. To pay membership dues
5. Other obligations specified in the operating policies

④ Associate members who do not register for the following semester are removed from membership.

**Article 6 (Full Members)**  
① An associate member who registers again for the following semester becomes a full member.  
② Full members have the following rights:

1. The rights of associate members under Article 5, paragraph 2
2. To vote in elections
3. To stand for election
4. Other rights specified in the operating policies

③ Full members have the following obligations:

1. The obligations of associate members under Article 5, paragraph 3
2. Other obligations specified in the operating policies

**Article 7 (Inactive Members)**  
① A full member who does not apply for OB status and does not register for the following semester becomes an inactive member.  
② An inactive member who registers again returns to full membership.  
③ An inactive member who does not register for four consecutive semesters is removed from membership.

**Article 8 (OB)**  
① A full member satisfying both conditions below may apply for OB status in the following semester:

1. Having registered as a full member for at least four semesters
2. Having graduated from an undergraduate program

② OB members are not removed from membership.  
③ OB members may return to full membership at any time by expressing their intention to return and paying membership dues.

## Chapter 3. Executives

**Article 9 (Composition)**  
① Administrative authority belongs to the executives.  
② The executives include the following required positions:

1. President
2. Vice President
3. Treasurer
4. Administrator

③ The President may appoint additional general executives or departments as necessary for club operations.  
④ Executives shall faithfully perform their respective duties, with the President bearing overall responsibility.

**Article 10 (President)**  
① The President represents the club and oversees its internal and external affairs.  
② The President has the following obligations:

1. To implement these rules in practice
2. To maintain and develop the club
3. To promote harmony among members

③ The President has the following rights:

1. To appoint the Vice President, Treasurer, general executives, and Administrator
2. To dismiss general executives
3. To appoint an Administrator when that position is vacant

**Article 11 (Vice President)**  
① The Vice President assists the President and performs the President's duties in their absence.

**Article 12 (Treasurer)**  
① The Treasurer handles all club financial affairs, and the independence of their duties is guaranteed.

**Article 13 (Administrator)**  
① The Administrator oversees the club's technical assets and information infrastructure and performs the following duties:

1. Operating and maintaining the website
2. Establishing and managing server and network infrastructure
3. Maintaining and operating IT equipment

② The Administrator has the following authority:

1. To designate the next Administrator
2. To manage club information as required for operations

**Article 14 (Executive Obligations)**  
① Executives have the following obligations to support the club's operation and development:

1. Managing and protecting club room equipment
2. Maintaining the cleanliness of the club room
3. Promoting member welfare and improving the environment
4. Maintaining technical infrastructure and managing its security
5. Other obligations specified in the operating policies

② Executives shall cooperate in carrying out their obligations, with the President bearing overall responsibility.

**Article 14-2 (Special Financial Provisions for Executives)**  
① Executives may receive a full refund of their membership dues when they are deemed to have faithfully performed their duties.  
② Refunded dues shall be clearly recorded in the financial report.

**Article 15 (Restrictions on Concurrent Positions)**  
① In principle, executives may not hold multiple positions concurrently.  
② Exceptions may be made in the following cases:

1. The Vice President also serves as Treasurer
2. The President considers concurrent service necessary and obtains the executives' consent

### Chapter 5. Meetings

**Article 16 (Purpose)**  
The club convenes the following meetings to achieve its objectives.

**Article 17 (General Meetings)**  
① The President chairs general meetings.  
② Regular general meetings are held as follows:

1. An opening general meeting around the start of each semester
2. A closing general meeting around the end of each semester

③ The President convenes general meetings and shall notify members at least seven days before the meeting date.  
④ The President shall announce decisions made at general meetings to all members.  
⑤ The opening general meeting addresses:

1. Collecting member suggestions
2. Adopting the semester's operating policies
3. Determining the direction of club operations

⑥ The closing general meeting addresses:

1. Collecting member suggestions
2. Reporting the semester's financial accounts
3. Electing the executives for the next semester

⑦ The following matters require a resolution of a general meeting:

1. Amendments to these rules
2. Disciplinary action against members
3. Matters the President considers significant

⑧ The President may convene an extraordinary general meeting with the consent of all executives when a matter is considered urgent or significant.  
⑨ The President shall convene an extraordinary general meeting when requested by a majority of the membership.  
⑩ Other details required to conduct a general meeting may be decided before it begins with the consent of a majority of those present.  
⑪ Members shall faithfully comply with resolutions adopted at general meetings.

**Article 18 (Executive Meetings)**  
① The President chairs executive meetings.  
② The President shall regularly convene executive meetings for the smooth operation of the club.  
③ The President shall convene an extraordinary executive meeting when requested by the executives.  
④ Members may ask the President to have their opinions discussed at an executive meeting.  
⑤ In principle, the proceedings of executive meetings are publicly available.

- However, the proceedings may exceptionally be withheld or disclosed with redactions in the following cases:
  1. When personal information may be exposed
  2. When disclosure may create a security risk
  3. When other legitimate grounds are recognized

⑥ Detailed procedures and other matters concerning executive meetings are determined by agreement among the executives.

### Chapter 6. Finances

**Article 19 (Sources of Funds)**  
① Club finances are funded by:

1. Membership dues
2. Official grants from external organizations and the university
3. Donations and other income

② All finances are managed under the Treasurer's responsibility, and the President may supervise them as necessary.

**Article 20 (Membership Dues)**  
① Members shall pay membership dues when registering each semester.  
② The amount and payment deadline generally follow the operating policies and are finally announced by the executives after discussion.  
③ Additional costs for special activities or events may be assessed separately and charged only to participating members.

**Article 21 (Refunds)**  
① In principle, paid membership dues are not refundable.  
② A refund may be requested only in the following cases:

1. Duplicate payment of dues
2. Withdrawal for a legitimate reason after payment but before club activities begin

③ When a refund is approved, the reason and amount shall be clearly recorded in the financial records.

**Article 22 (Treasurer's Responsibilities and Transparency)**  
① The Treasurer shall accurately record all club income and expenditure according to the following principles:

1. Thorough retention of receipts and other supporting documents
2. Regular recording and review of all income and expenditure
3. Management of expenditure against the budget

② The Treasurer shall make financial records immediately available for inspection when requested by the President.  
③ The Treasurer shall regularly prepare financial reports under these rules and submit them to the executives and general meetings.  
④ The Treasurer shall not execute financial transactions without instructions from others and shall follow approval procedures involving the President or executives.

**Article 23 (Financial Reports)**  
① The Treasurer shall prepare and publish a financial report before each semester's general meeting.  
② Financial reports shall include:

1. Details of income and expenditure
2. Budgeted and actual expenditure for each item
3. Funds carried forward and cash holdings
4. Refunds, donations, and other notable matters

③ Financial reports shall be available to all members at executive and general meetings, and the President may review and question the Treasurer's report.

**Article 24 (Approval of Expenditure)**  
① Expenditure exceeding KRW 300,000 may not be authorized by the Treasurer alone and requires one of the following procedures:

1. Prior approval by the President
2. Approval at an executive meeting

② Large budgets for specific purposes, such as events or projects, shall be announced before expenditure.  
③ Funds spent in violation of these procedures without legitimate grounds may be recovered from the individual responsible.

### Chapter 7. Operations and Activities

**Article 25 (Definition of SIG)**  
① A group of club members sharing an interest in a particular topic is called a SIG (Special Interest Group).  
② In principle, any topic is permitted. However, establishment of a SIG is restricted in the following cases:

1. Topics restricted by the operating policies
2. Topics considered inappropriate by a majority of the executives

③ SIGs may receive grants. Grant amounts and eligibility conditions are specified in the operating policies.  
④ Club members may establish SIGs.  
⑤ In principle, the President determines the SIG enrollment period and shall announce the standard enrollment period at least three days before it ends.  
⑥ Club members may join SIGs during their enrollment periods.

**Article 26 (SIG Leaders)**  
① SIG leaders are defined as follows:

1. The founder of a SIG becomes its first leader.
2. A person who receives leadership from the previous leader becomes the leader, and the previous leader becomes an ordinary member of the SIG.

② SIG leaders have the following rights:

1. To allow enrollment throughout the semester. In this case, the enrollment period under Article 25, paragraph 5 is the entire semester.

**Article 27 (SIG Classification)**  
① SIGs are classified according to the following criteria:

1. SIGs dealing with computing topics and primarily focused on studying or practicing those topics are classified as computing-related SIGs.
2. Among SIGs falling under item 2, those requiring unrestricted budget support, such as projects, are classified as PIGs (Project Interest Groups).
3. SIGs falling under neither item 1 nor item 2 are classified as informal-group SIGs.

② The President shall work to establish introductory SIGs for new members each semester.  
③ With the executives' agreement, the President shall announce each semester's classifications within one week after the SIG recruitment period ends. The period and method for objections shall be announced at the same time.  
④ An objection to a classification may be submitted when at least one of the following conditions is met:

1. Supplementary material containing a concrete activity plan on a computing-related topic is submitted
2. An omission or significant error has occurred through a system issue or mistake
3. A change to an informal-group SIG is requested

**Article 28 (Seminars)**  
① A seminar is a temporary gathering of club members interested in a topic to study or share information.  
② Seminars may address any computing-related topic and may be freely organized on members' suggestions.  
③ Seminars may receive support under the operating policies, and spending shall follow financial approval procedures.

**Article 29 (MT)**  
① MT (Membership Training) is an activity to promote friendship among members and may be organized by the executives.  
② Participation is voluntary and shall not be compulsory.  
③ MT may receive budget support under the operating policies and requires approval by the President and executives when necessary.

**Article 30 (Club Room Cleaning)**  
① The executives organize major cleaning sessions to maintain the cleanliness and condition of the club room.  
② The President shall ensure that at least one major cleaning session takes place during each summer and winter term.  
③ Cleaning expenses are funded from the budget under the operating policies.

# Chapter 8. Discipline and Impeachment

**Article 31 (Discipline)**  
① A member may be subject to disciplinary action in the following cases:

1. Committing a crime
2. Damaging club property or causing financial loss to the club

② A disciplinary committee may be convened when either condition is met:

1. A majority of full members agree
2. All executives other than the person concerned agree

③ The disciplinary committee is composed as follows:

1. Normally, the President, Vice President, and three full members
2. If the President or Vice President is subject to discipline, five full members

④ Full members serving on the committee shall have no direct conflict of interest involving the accused or the victim.  
⑤ The committee shall thoroughly examine the relevant materials and disclose its findings to members. Sensitive information, including personal information, shall be withheld or disclosed anonymously. The committee shall also prepare and submit an official verdict.  
⑥ Final disciplinary action is determined by a resolution of a general meeting.

**Article 32 (Impeachment)**  
① An executive may be subject to impeachment for any of the following:

1. Conduct described in Article 31, paragraph 1
2. Serious neglect of duties or failure to fulfill obligations
3. Causing substantial harm to the club

② Impeachment follows the same procedures as disciplinary action.

# Supplementary Provisions

**Article 1 (Definition of a Semester)**  
① Summer and winter terms are considered extensions of the spring and fall semesters, respectively.  
② Therefore, one semester is defined as either:

1. Spring + summer
2. Fall + winter

**Article 2 (Scope of Seoul National University)**  
① In these rules, Seoul National University includes all colleges and graduate programs operated by the Seoul National University corporation.

**Article 3 (Effective Date)**  
① These rules take effect on September 1, 2025 and remain in force until the next amendment.

**Article 4 (Interpretation of Unspecified Matters)**  
① Matters not specified in these rules or whose interpretation is unclear shall follow these principles:

1. Ordinary rules
2. Club practices
3. Common sense and generally accepted social norms
